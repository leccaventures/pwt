refactor(processor): split processBlock and improve block fetch retry policy

- Split processBlock into single-responsibility helpers
  - resolveBlockHeight, ensureProofNode, getBlockTimeAndUpdateMetrics
  - fetchAndCacheBlockTime, updateExporterBlockMetrics, fetchBlockProofWithRetry
  - buildSignedSet, updateValidatorsFromProof, logProofFetchError

- Treat "not found" as retryable (same as "block is not available")
